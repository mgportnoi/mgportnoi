## About Me

Software Engineer and QA Analyst with a strong foundation in Software Engineering, Requirements Engineering, and Digital Product Development.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, a postgraduate degree in Software Engineering, and I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Computer Science, focusing on Experimental Software Engineering. My background combines hands-on industry experience with applied research aimed at improving software quality.

I have practical experience as a Junior QA Analyst, working with functional and non-functional testing for Web, Mobile, and API-based systems. My activities include test analysis and specification, manual and automated test execution, defect reporting, post-deployment validation, and collaboration with developers and product owners in agile environments.

I also have experience as a Software Engineer, contributing to the development and evolution of web systems using Java, JavaScript, and C#. My work includes bug fixing, refactoring, automated testing, database integration, and adherence to best practices in version control, code quality, and agile methodologies.

In parallel, I have worked as a Product Owner and Requirements Analyst, conducting requirement elicitation, documentation, prioritization, and validation, ensuring alignment between business goals, user needs, and technical feasibility.

My academic research explores the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) to support software engineering tasks, particularly automated test generation, requirements specification, and empirical evaluation of software quality using metrics such as code coverage, mutation testing, readability, and reliability.
